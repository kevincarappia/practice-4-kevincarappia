Slow BBQ Chicken

Low Heat, a lid, and sauce added late so it never burns

Ingredients

  • 8 chicken thighs, bone in
  • 1 tbsp smoke paprika
  • 2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 cup barbecue sauce
  • 1 tbsp cider vinegar
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Method

  1. Heat in the oven
  2. Season the chicken
  3. Bake Covered
  4. Sauce and Finish
  5. Rest and Serve
1

Set it to 325 degrees. Low and slow is the whole trick
